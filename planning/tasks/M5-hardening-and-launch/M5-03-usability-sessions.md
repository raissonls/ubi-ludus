# M5-03 · Usability sessions

**Milestone:** M5 Hardening and launch (Dec 14-20) · **Priority:** Must · **Estimate:** 3 h

## Why

Check with real people that first-time use is fast and unaided.

## Definition of done

- [ ] At least 5 volunteers (family and friends), timed with a stopwatch.
- [ ] First-time visitor finds their team's next match and where to watch in 30 s or less; returning visitor in 10 s or less.
- [ ] At least 4 of 5 complete onboarding unaided.
- [ ] Findings and fixes logged.
