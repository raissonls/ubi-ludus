# ADR-NNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related:** issues, PRD requirements, other ADRs

## Context

What problem or constraint forces a decision? Facts only: what we know, what limits us.

## Decision

What we decided, in plain sentences. If there are several linked decisions, number them.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and which risks we accept.

## Alternatives considered

What else we looked at and why we did not choose it.
